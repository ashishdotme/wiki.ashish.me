# Dahi Kadhi (Maharashtrian Style)

[Video: Mangal Recipe's Marathi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=utCVs7jVyCg)

## Ingredients

### Kadhi base

- Curd/yogurt (dahi) - 4 tbsp (sour preferred)
- Besan (gram flour) - 2 tbsp
- Water - 2 cups
- Salt - to taste
- Turmeric powder - 1/4 tsp

### Tadka (tempering)

- Oil/ghee - 1 tbsp
- Mustard seeds - 1/2 tsp
- Cumin seeds - 1/2 tsp
- Curry leaves - 8-10
- Dry red chillies - 2
- Asafoetida (hing) - a pinch
- Green chillies - 2, slit
- Garlic - 3-4 cloves, crushed (optional)

## Steps

1. Whisk curd, besan, turmeric, salt, and water together until smooth with no lumps
2. Heat oil/ghee in a pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter
3. Add cumin seeds, curry leaves, dry red chillies, hing, green chillies, and garlic. Saute briefly
4. Pour the curd-besan mixture into the pan
5. Stir continuously on medium heat to prevent curdling
6. Bring to a boil while stirring, then simmer on low for 10-15 minutes until kadhi thickens slightly
7. Serve hot with steamed rice
